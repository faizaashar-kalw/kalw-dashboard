We're looking at our audiences in two primary ways in this dashboard. One is trying to answer the question, "how many people come across KALW week by week, even if unknowingly?" and the other is trying to answer, "how many people intentionally take an action to engage with KALW?"

One way to think about this is, how many people do you see when you're out in the world versus how many people do you talk to? (Inspired by Faiza Ashar who built this dashboard!)

In 2025, our broadcast had an average reach of about 92,000 people who tune into KALW weekly. There was an average of nearly 2,500 people listening at any given time between 6–10 a.m. That gives us a sense of how many people are engaged during shows that air at that time.

A "download" counts anytime someone presses play on any platform and listens for 60 seconds or more. This includes podcast players, on a web browser or an app.
Total downloads
A show's volume and total consumption over the quarter.
UNIQUE LISTENERS
Amount of individual listeners that play an episode, counted only once regardless of how many times they play one. Essentially a show's following, the dedicated audience that stays engaged week by week.
The difference?
Unique listeners are counted only once in the time period. If I download a Crosscurrents episode every day one comes out, I still count as one unique listener. However, every one of those downloads lands in the total.

Combine assumptions + data.
Case study — Sights + Sounds
Goal & persona
What the data shows
The insight

Audience goal: Grow listenership.

Listener profile: Local Bay Area listeners tuning in after work and on weekends to unwind.

+80% Q1 to Q2 weekly download growth.

87% of podcast listeners are generally on mobile (via SQ Magazine)

12,000+ downloads via NPR platforms vs. under 900 on the second-highest player.

Shorter "Sights+Sounds Picks" segments outperform full episodes.

NPR's platforms drive growth, and listeners heavily favor shorter segments for their on-demand content. We can also assume that Sights + Sounds' audience are NPR listeners too.

03
Turn data insights into actionable tests.
Instead of starting from scratch, double down on what works and iterate.
On-air promotion
Add clear calls-to-action on air, directing listeners to NPR platforms.
Content formatting
Slice full shows into shorter, highlight-style clips.
Headline testing
Test different headline styles to see what drives higher download numbers on NPR.
Our broad question, narrowed
"Which headline formats drive the most downloads on the NPR app?"
Key takeaway
Pick one question. Start where you want to go, run a focused test, and replicate what works.
